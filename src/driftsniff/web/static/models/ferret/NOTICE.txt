The Driftsniff ferret (ferret.gltf, ferret.bin and the three .webp textures).

Generated for Theriark with Meshy (image-to-3D, paid plan: the output is owned by the subscriber with
no attribution required), then slimmed, painted, rigged and animated by Theriark in Blender. It is the
same model as the Theriark website's ferret, converted for this product with glTF-Transform 4: the
Draco mesh decoded and quantized (KHR_mesh_quantization), the textures kept as separate WebP files
(EXT_texture_webp) and only two clips kept, idle_sniff and look_around. Plain glTF, so it loads under
the product's content security policy without WebAssembly, workers or blob URLs.
